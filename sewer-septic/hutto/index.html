The Hutto angle

What Hutto soil does to sewer lines.

The ground under slab foundations on some of the most expansive clay soil in Central Texas moves with the weather. Wet months swell the ground, dry months shrink it, and buried sewer lines get pushed out of grade, cracked at the joints, or bellied where water and solids collect. Add PVC drain lines that the shifting clay can pull out of grade, plus older lines around downtown and you have the two causes behind most Hutto sewer failures.

We do not open a trench across your yard to find out which one you have. A camera inspection shows the break, the belly, or the roots, and we recommend the repair that matches, warranty-backed. Septic customers get the same straight answer on pumping, inspection, and drain field health.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What is trenchless sewer repair?+

Trenchless methods rehabilitate or replace your sewer line through one or two small access points instead of digging a trench across the whole yard. In established neighborhoods full of trees, patios, and slab foundations, that matters.

Do you tunnel under slabs in Hutto?+

Yes. When the break is under the foundation, precision tunneling reaches the line from outside so we are not breaking up floors inside your home. On slab foundations on some of the most expansive clay soil in Central Texas, that is often the least disruptive option, and we show you the camera footage before recommending it.

How often should my septic tank be pumped?+

Most household tanks need pumping every 3 to 5 years depending on size and usage. Slow drains everywhere, gurgling, odors, or soggy spots near the drain field mean it is due. We offer same-day septic pumping across Greater Austin.
